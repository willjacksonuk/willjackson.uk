# Context

A glossary of the terms used for this site's content. It describes what
things are, not how they're built.

**Project**
A piece of work shown on the site. Every project has a kind and a case study.

**Kind**
What sort of project it is. There are two kinds:
- **Website**: a public site, usually with a live link.
- **Tool**: an internal tool, system or process, usually not public.

**Featured project**
The one project shown large at the top of the Work section. It's currently
Woven & Woods.

**Case study**
The long page about one project, under /work/. Each case study ends with a
link to the next project.

**Proof point**
A single verified figure shown on the home page. There's currently only one:
95% organic search growth over three years at Woven & Woods. Don't add
figures that haven't been checked.
